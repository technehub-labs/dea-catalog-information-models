## 3. The chain lens

Each model records its stage on the Agreement → Offering → Product → Service → Resource → Entity chain (derived from the BO's object class): legal instruments → agreements; offerings → offering; transactions → service/delivery; resources/locations → resource; technology-control → entity/support; parties and work items → party lifecycle; financial instruments → financial. Chain participation is carried as `chain_stage` on the artifact and in record tags, and shapes the model's structural attributes (e.g. agreement models carry counterparty binding; resource models carry supported-service references).

## 4. Evidence

Two-part per CR-IM-CAT-03: provenance = the canonical BO record (system of record; its definition, state history, and capabilities/processes references are the semantic source — no external-standard anchors, per CR-IM-CAT-05) + the committed artifact. `dea:structures` assertions remain deferred until downstream DataEntity/InformationAsset stand-ups shaped by these models.
